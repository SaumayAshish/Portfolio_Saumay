MISSION SUMMARY · WHY / WHAT / HOW / CONCLUSION
WHY
How much to stock and whether equipment is about to fail are both usually answered with a rule of thumb.
WHAT
Two ML systems: per-store demand forecasting over 1,115 stores, and remaining-useful-life prediction for jet engines on NASA C-MAPSS.
HOW
A baseline ladder up to a Temporal Fusion Transformer, 6 compared architectures plus an autoencoder, FastAPI + Streamlit, MLflow, DVC, Docker.
CONCLUSION
Forecast error cut from 31.4% to 9.4% WAPE; degrading engines flagged 70–100 cycles early. Offline results, not deployed.
