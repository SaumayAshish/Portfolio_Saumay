MISSION SUMMARY · WHY / WHAT / HOW / CONCLUSION
WHY
Knowledge scatters across tools, and most AI note apps lock your notes in and send raw content to the cloud.
WHAT
An Obsidian-first system: Markdown stays the source of truth, a Python layer syncs structured records to PostgreSQL, and a local RAG pipeline answers questions.
HOW
Semantic chunking, local embeddings and context compression, never persisting raw note bodies. CI with automated fix PRs and a phased pgvector plan.
CONCLUSION
Notes stay owned and portable while becoming searchable, with a scaling path that does not break existing users.
